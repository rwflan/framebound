# Muse animation reference research

Research gathered 2026-10-30 for the [Muse animation corpus plan](../../MUSE_ANIMATION_CORPUS_PLAN.md).

Open [index.html](index.html) for a local gallery organized by the 48 proposed styles. The original downloaded PNG/JPEG/GIF files live in `references/`. No images were generated by this research task, and these files are not benchmark outputs, approved style packs or human-accepted training positives.

- [reference-images.json](reference-images.json): 273 byte-deduplicated downloaded images, original URLs, local files, SHA-256, dimensions/frame counts, source IDs and proposed style leads.
- [reference-sources.json](reference-sources.json): 142 source records; 76 creator/asset-page leads (one unavailable) and 66 museum records. Unavailable and differently licensed source leads remain explicit. Page/record hashes identify observed metadata; they are not proof of image-generation origin.
- [style-matrix.json](style-matrix.json): 36 core styles plus 12 extensions, proposed native sizes, palette/trait/motion requirements and actual seed-reference IDs.
- [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md): candidate-file counts and acquisition gaps. No style is generation-ready before its conditioning pack is curated.
- [acquisition-queue.json](acquisition-queue.json): explicit per-style role/family/image gaps and acquisition briefs before generation.
- [verification.json](verification.json): offline byte-hash, decode/size and local-gallery-link checks.
- [model-research.json](model-research.json): read-only Muse tooling observations and primary model-documentation sources. No account configuration, credential or model request is included.

## Reference rights and actual roles

Downloaded game-art references came from asset pages declaring CC0. Museum images have an individual CC0 declaration in the Cleveland Museum of Art's API. Source-level declarations and creator credits are retained; see [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and the museum's [Open Access policy](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access). These source declarations are recorded evidence, not an assertion that every pixel of a promotional screenshot—including logos—has been curated for transmission. Licence scope and original reference credit remain attached to the images rather than being replaced by the repository code licence.

Do not assume the rest of a site, all artwork by an author, or a pack's promotional branding inherits an asset's CC0 declaration. The CGA source lead has a different licence and was retained as a research link without downloading its images. The missing Kenney pack remains a failed source lookup. No source is silently mirrored from an unrelated uploader after a failed fetch.

Sprite sheets, preview GIFs and material studies have different roles. A museum still does not establish motion, an unlabelled sprite preview does not establish authoritative timing, and several crops/resolutions of one sheet are one family. Proposed style assignments are leads, not completed visual suitability judgments. Keep `conditioning_status=not_curated` until role, source/family, overlays, relevance and model transmission choices are actually reviewed.

## Reuse and refresh

Resolve image IDs through the manifest; use the original bytes and verify their full SHA-256 before import. Refresh from the recorded source URL only if necessary. If bytes or publisher declarations change, create a new source/asset version and retain the previous evidence; do not overwrite it to make a check pass. Derived crops or palette/size transformations need their own hashes and parent/operation records.

A pack becomes ready only after the plan's minimum 12 relevant files, three independent source/character families and appearance/motion/material role coverage are met. The seed count is deliberately separate from this readiness gate. Remaining exact-style and motion examples must be acquired before that style enters the scored Muse corpus.

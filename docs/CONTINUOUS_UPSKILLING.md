# Continuous evidence-led upskilling

The operating objective is broader subject/action coverage and more reliable,
scoped knowledge. GPU compute is admitted when its measured value justifies it.
Installing this controller does not establish model accuracy, artistic acceptance
or completion of the research stages.

## What the earlier pilots established

The first source recipe failed acquisition too often. An opaque-key source recipe
with explicit per-cell margins improved its small rehearsal, but the higher
complexity probe still exposed some unsuitable sources and a generation timeout.
Sparse independently corroborated positives also prevented meaningful local
baseline comparisons. More generic generation alone is therefore insufficient.
Actual experiments, artwork, prices/account declarations, receipts and their
counts live outside Git; source code contains no participant data.

## 1. Resolve operational blockers without inventing provider outcomes

`campaign isolate-unknown --request FILE` takes `request_id` and `evidence` with
`source` and `reason`. It records a permanent research exclusion for an unknown
request that has no recoverable acceptance ID. Its provider state remains unknown,
its full reservation stays booked, and its family and reference ancestry cannot
create another paid attempt. The campaign remains stopped. This is neither a
receipt nor a refund. Isolated families are excluded from newly derived training
features and advisory knowledge. Old snapshots and annotations are preserved.

Independent research may use genuinely new families and a new predeclared recipe.
It cannot replace the unknown specimen, reuse its anchor or ancestry, move its
split, reset costs or claim that the blocked pilot passed. Existing lifetime
concurrency and budget reservations still apply across campaigns.

New independent curricula use `stream-images-v1`: one native Meta image POST with
`stream: true`, `store: true`, a deterministic binding tag and bounded conditioning
previews. Original references are retained; previews preserve aspect ratio, use
LANCZOS and have a longest edge of 256 pixels. Every preview records its input and
output hashes, sizes and recipe in the frozen request.

The `response.created` event commits provider identity before the terminal image
arrives. A creation event can omit model or metadata; the ID remains bound to its
own original POST. Terminal model and response identity are validated, and any
returned binding metadata must match. Complete streams and interrupted prefixes
are retained as observations; canonical terminal response extraction is recorded
as a derivation. A restart only GETs the accepted ID, never repeats creation.
Missing acknowledgement remains unknown and is never retried. Pending responses
and reservations still block stage release. No historical ID is reconstructed.

The separate `background-images-v1` opt-in is retained for compatible provider
routes, with its 1 MiB body limit and strict queued acknowledgement. The live Meta
image background route rejected the documented image tool during pre-inference
validation, so it is not the default continuation transport. That actual rejection
was preserved with zero output cost. Native Responses streaming subsequently
produced a real image, retained its ID/transcript and passed read-only retrieval.
Provider capability checks are engineering evidence, not semantic training data.

## 2. Freeze a new breadth and depth curriculum

`campaign plan-upskill --root PRIVATE_ROOT --campaign NAME` freezes an independent
MVP v2 study. It retains 800 specifications, 200 families, 480 train / 160 validation
/ 160 final cases, the twelve-case rehearsal and eighty-case pilot. A stable study
namespace and explicit subject designs/palettes/silhouettes distinguish new family
identities. Characters, animals, figures and objects span eight treatments, five
complexity levels, four exercises, legitimate counterexamples and 6/12/24 poses.
All scopes remain isolated 2D, fixed camera and topology, with optional held props.

The independent-coverage assessment recipe runs a second, blinded assessment even
on negative examples. It supplies stable subject names, requires actual native
scope and prioritizes temporal/artifact coverage while retaining all nine taxonomy
categories. It never requests positive labels to fill a quota. Disagreements,
unsupported localization and missing scope remain masked or unassessed. The same
strict corroboration rules still control positive training weights and edit
permission. A second execution is not human truth.

Historical v1 and v2 configurations retain byte-identical serialized defaults;
the new transport and assessment policies are explicit opt-ins.

`plan-upskill` also accepts a request with `source_policy: single-anchor-keyed-v2`
for a new independent study after diagnosed acquisition failures. This opt-in
freezes disjoint families and separates the one-pose neutral anchor brief from
the animation exercise. Every sheet brief specifies a central 256-square drawing
region in each 512-square cell. Generation conditioning previews explicitly
composite retained alpha onto the declared magenta key, recording that derivation;
semantic teachers still receive original alpha frames. Teacher briefs supply the
authored tick bounds and require nonempty observed-scope limitations. Neither
receipt replay nor intake/semantic validation changes. An earlier failed cohort
stays failed; a new recipe must independently pass rehearsal and the full pilot.

After a diagnosed sheet-rule failure, a new independent study may opt into
`source_policy: declared-sheet-rules-v3`. Its frozen brief explicitly reserves
uniform white outer cell-edge rules as technical separators, at most eight pixels
after cell scaling, immediately followed by the declared magenta key. Only this
policy permits their recorded removal. Nonuniform edges, wider mattes, wrong keys
and interior boxes still fail; alpha/isolation thresholds are unchanged. Originals
and transformations remain preserved. Anchor canvases have no separators. Teacher
briefs explicitly tie every finding slot to overlapping authored ticks and list
all required finding fields. Old rejected sources and quarantined answers are not
reprocessed into passing outcomes; the new families start a separate rehearsal.

The declared-rule decoder requires an observed near-white rule before accepting
its narrow white-to-magenta resampling transition. The existing eight-pixel band,
inward-key proof and alpha gate still apply. Pink-only borders, wrong keys and
wide mattes remain unsuitable. Record the edge recipe in the derivation; never
silently reinterpret prior closed rejections after a decoder correction.

`native-layout-key-v4` is a separate opt-in acquisition experiment. It derives
an explicitly recorded six-cell placement template from the family's isolated
identity anchor; the repeated reference poses guide size/position and never enter
sequence labels or supervised output frames. Native cell extraction precedes resizing,
so downsampling cannot destroy separator evidence. The native rule allowance is
scaled to the same eight canonical pixels, and the resulting256-square alpha
frames still pass the unchanged isolation gate. Earlier policies retain their
processing order and frozen outputs. Source failures remain failures.

## 3. Execute fixed, restartable batches

`campaign upskill-plan` reports the next admissible action without funding or
network calls. `campaign upskill-step --muse --request STEP.json` performs one
explicit live step. A request looks like:

```json
{
  "batch": "batch-001",
  "max_cases": 12,
  "allocation_microdollars": 2000000,
  "evidence": {
    "verified_at": "<actual current timezone-aware verification>",
    "source": "<verified standard pricing>",
    "account_access": "<actual account evidence and operator allocation>",
    "image_microdollars": 10000
  }
}
```

At most twenty cases may enter one step. The controller saves the exact case IDs,
policy and configuration hash before collection. Repeating an interrupted step
reuses the same cohort, consumes received results, and retrieves accepted pending
responses. It cannot silently select twenty additional cases. Completed step
replays do not fund, call providers or re-export data. Changed step requests are
rejected. CLI execution is required; HTTP cannot invoke a live specialist step.
Generation provenance hashes the actual frozen paid brief, so changing the
selected advisory library cannot rewrite a received repair's submission binding.

After a diagnosed execution-contract fix, an explicitly resumed campaign can use
a new batch with `recovery_of` naming its blocked parent. The configuration,
maximum size and exact original case IDs must match. Terminal cases stay intact;
unfinished cases consume their existing paid receipts. A recovery cannot replenish
a completed unhealthy cohort, select other cases, resolve unknown acceptance or
release another stage. It closes a new dataset version and leaves the blocked
report and published snapshot unchanged. Only a completed recovery that passes
the same cohort gate creates a checked resolution link; replay of the parent then
returns that completed recovery without funding or provider calls.

Comparators receive the exact enum/schema for model-authored fields; local
assignment and provenance fields are never requested from the model. A valid
provider response with invalid comparison fields is preserved and quarantined.
It produces an explicit insufficient-evidence comparison with no assessed
categories and unknown improvement/regression. Qualified category strings are
never normalized into valid labels. Empty scope is forbidden for any claimed
preference or outcome. Such abstentions cannot approve repairs or create binary
preference/editing supervision; malformed provider envelopes still stop admission.

Funding is cumulative, distinct from its ceiling, and requires current account
and rate evidence. The participant's aggregate authorization includes historical
costs and unresolved reservations across all campaigns. No action resets spend.
Release, funding and actual request reservation each retain their own checks.

### Continue unaffected work after an isolated transport interruption

`campaign drain-isolated --muse --request FILE` finishes only unaffected members
of an existing frozen cohort. The request names `parent_batch`, an immutable new
`version`, and at most20 `max_cases`. It requires the parent's actual interrupted
report, matching configuration and exact case list, permanent isolation of every
unknown request, and no active accepted response. It cannot replenish a failed
source cohort, retry the unknown family, select replacement cases or release a
stage. The original cohort remains incomplete and its original denominator and
75%/25% gates remain unchanged. The parent report stays immutable; the campaign
returns to stopped after the bounded continuation, including on exceptions.

If two unknown assessment jobs occupy the default two slots, an explicit local
`recovery-capacity` amendment can admit one additional assessment slot, expiring
within24hours. It requires current account/provider-capacity evidence and keeps
ALL unknown jobs counted: two uncertain jobs plus one active job equals the
three-slot bound. It does not cancel or declare any old job finished. Budget,
generation concurrency, request timeouts, family quarantine and scientific
configuration remain unchanged. These operations are CLI-only and never an
implicit consequence of planning or querying status.

After every unaffected member of that interrupted batch has closed, an explicit
`continue-admitted` request may collect up to20 remaining cases from the SAME
already-released pilot. It binds `parent_version`, a new immutable `version`,
the original pilot release and the unchanged configuration. The previous cohort
must still meet its original scoped-coverage, source and receipt thresholds;
only permanently isolated unknown members may remain incomplete. The new fixed
case list is frozen before dispatch, selected from unfinished admitted pilot
cases without replacing the old unknown specimen. No expansion or final cases
are admitted and no old report, denominator or outcome is rewritten. Full pilot
release remains blocked until the original full-cohort requirements are met.

A received repair or blind-rerun source that fails strict isolation is retained
as a candidate acquisition failure, without submitting or assessing that candidate.
The valid baseline and its scoped labels remain intact; the failed repair stays
ambiguous and its authority closes without approval. There is no replacement
generation or comparator. Allocated control arms retain provider failure or
explicitly missing outcomes rather than fabricated preferences. Source and
candidate failures together must remain within the same 25% cohort acquisition
allowance. Receipt/shape/schema errors still stop admission, and a blocked batch
requires explicit diagnosis and exact-cohort recovery before continuation.

Each completed batch closes an immutable dataset, generates matched numeric and
geometry features, saves baseline support reports and verifies a train-only
advisory library. The library is selected only for already authorized correction
briefs; independent assessment and blind comparisons never receive it. The batch
receives its own receipt/usable-scope/intake audit. An unhealthy batch stops before
another paid tranche. Full pilot and expansion gates remain unchanged. Final-test
cases are never selected by this development controller.

Progress is under `campaigns/NAME/upskill/active.json`; completed evidence is in
`latest.json` and each batch directory. While a provider response is accepted but
pending, the status is `waiting_for_response`, not a failed artistic outcome.
Use the original saved step request to continue it. A stopped campaign or an
unknown request requires diagnosis and cannot auto-advance.

## 4. Spend against evidence gaps

Use staged allocations within the authorized aggregate ceiling. First complete
the rehearsal; then collect the pilot in twenty-case checkpoints. Only a passing
full eighty-case pilot permits representative expansion tranches of at most eighty
new cases, still consumed in twenty-case batches. Selection is outcome-blind and
retains its original inclusion probability. Adaptive sampling continues to require
an informative baseline.

Prioritize missing identity families and strata, then independently corroborated
positive and assessed negative support. Do not inflate coverage with variants or
repeatedly assess obvious clean examples. Acquisition failures remain separate
negative acquisition evidence, not semantic targets. If source reliability or
teacher scope fails, diagnose that cause before further collection. If the
available curriculum is exhausted without support, report that limitation rather
than weaken thresholds or manufacture labels.

The controller compares numeric and geometry features using identical closed
data, labels and splits. It marks a comparison uninformative when support is
insufficient. When the joint temporal/artifact baseline is unsupported, it checks
each category separately. A category can become a narrowly advertised candidate
without falsely declaring unsupported categories trained.

## 5. Make the compute decision measurable

An informative category gets an actual CPU/MPS workload profile. A supported
training candidate still needs 300 distinct training sequences, 75 families and
30 corroborated positives plus 30 assessed negatives for every advertised category.
Local fitting does not select or promote a model. Validation calibration and
matched repair-versus-blind-rerun evidence remain required for promotion and final
release.

Batch closure automatically runs the supported local training experiment when
its category and data gates pass. It saves both model recipes across the frozen
seeds, checkpoints, validation calibration and a model card; no operator handoff
is required to start local training. Category sets have separate checkpoint
directories. Models remain unpromoted pending the existing release evidence.

The result's `compute_candidate` supplies the category-specific baseline, feature
file, training support and local profile. Use the existing `compute-plan` with
these exact artifacts, current applicable credit/pricing evidence, a substantive
hypothesis and a pinned worker image. Only canonical numeric features can cross
the Modal wire. Pixels, artwork, prompts and credentials have no transfer field.
The plan reports missing evidence without dispatching a job.

The initial measured experiment remains bounded: up to $1 for profiling, $3 for
training and $2 for reproduction, including earlier bookings. A received matching
remote profile must show at least 25% end-to-end speedup over the fastest successful
local device before remote training. Known accepted remote jobs replay; unknown
acceptance retains its reservation and never retries. Credit expiry, applicable
balance, workspace caps, aggregate wall allowance and zero uncovered cash spend
remain binding. More available credit is not a reason to change these gates.

Small feature models may fit locally more efficiently. If that is what the
measurements show, retain the credit. A larger representation or search recipe
requires an explicit versioned experiment, matching local ablation and a compatible
validated wire format before GPU spend is justified.

## 6. Keep execution moving and reporting honest

A private bounded supervisor can repeatedly execute saved steps while budget and
stage gates hold, using GET-only recovery for accepted responses. Schedule one
follow-up to inspect its durable progress, continue a healthy next stage or
address a concrete blocker. Do not duplicate an active worker. Notify on meaningful
completion, failure, stage transition or required input; ordinary active polling
stays quiet. All private experiment plans and reports remain outside Git.

Primary provider references: [Meta background responses and retrieval](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/protocols/responses),
[Meta standard pricing](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/pricing-rate-limits).

## Explicit segmented-source acquisition

The opt-in `sam-isolation-v5` source policy declares flat technical backing and
model-derived alpha before any semantic assessment. It uses disjoint frozen
families, one neutral anchor and a placement template. One stored native streamed
`sam-3.1` still-image request segments each received anchor or six-cell sheet.
Install the `campaign-acquisition` extra for the official parser pinned by commit;
SAM's compressed protocol is not generic base85 or a packed bitmap. The parser
normalizes inclusive boxes to half-open bounds, and any box-local mask scaling is
recorded explicitly before image resizing.

Each request is separately quoted using `segmentation_microdollars` and requires
matching current funding evidence. Its original response, acceptance identity,
mask hashes and source binding are retained. Replays consume the same receipt;
pending acceptance resumes by GET, and unknown acceptance is never retried.
Missing/duplicate masks, wrong dimensions, cross-cell/clipped subjects and scenic
surroundings fail acquisition. At least 98% of unselected pixels in every cell
must match the declared white or magenta technical backing, with at least 25%
surroundings. This predeclared policy does not rehabilitate earlier failed media.
The original strict alpha intake, scoped semantic assessment, corroboration,
75% usable scope and 25% acquisition-failure gates still apply. A model mask is
structural evidence, never artistic acceptance or automatic training readiness.
SAM concepts use the concrete design noun already frozen in the source brief
(for example, `folding umbrella`), rather than the curriculum's broad `object`
category. Completed empty output is a retained no-match acquisition failure;
changing the selector cannot purchase a replacement mask for a received request.

`sam-spatial-v6` is a separate source experiment when class-conditioned masks
miss clearly drawn subjects. Its sole SAM request uses the documented object-prompt
mode: one native box around observed drawing pixels in each declared cell, positive
points on actual nontechnical ink, and negative points on verified technical
surroundings. It never combines a noun prompt with object tokens or assumes an
empty cell center is foreground. These proposals do not confer semantic labels. Boxes, coordinates, source size and object IDs are frozen in the
request and retained in derivation provenance. Mask IDs must map back to their
own cells. The original strict extraction/alpha, scene exclusion, scoped assessment,
corroboration, receipt, budget and release gates remain unchanged. A prior received
no-match is not re-requested or turned into an accepted specimen.

Split quarantine uses the 64-bit luminance hash only to find near-duplicate
candidates. Common placement layouts can collide despite different drawings.
`confirmed-visual-quarantine-v2` confirms candidates using detailed visible pixels
on black/white composites, measured over content rather than empty backing, with
small translation tolerance. Detailed occupancy/edge agreement also retains
recolored copies. Family/ancestry, exact shared-input and exact-file exclusions
remain absolute. Historical one-stage reports remain reproducible with
`legacy_exclusions`; immutable old exports, reports and libraries are not rewritten.
New feature source manifests record the quarantine policy and source hash; new
knowledge versions record the policy. A corrected learning projection must use
new version names over its original frozen dataset, never new labels or media.

## Native-cell acquisition contract

The opt-in `sam-native-cell-v7` freezes one still-image segmentation lane per
native sheet cell before any outcome is observed. Each of the six inputs is a
lossless crop of the original source, with its own bounded cell binding, cost,
receipt and source hash. An accepted response resumes by retrieval; a received
failed mask cannot switch methods or purchase a replacement. These six inputs
remain one source sequence, never six training examples.

All original cells first pass an observed-ink connected-component edge check,
so masking cannot hide a limb clipped by the source cell. Each mask retains the
existing native margin, technical-background and surroundings requirements.
Alpha is reassembled at native size before normal frame resizing and intake.
Generation receives recorded full-layout references at a maximum 512 pixels,
without competing enlarged single-subject references. Identity, baseline and
continuity originals remain retained; repeated reference poses are conditioning
only. Older source policies retain their original request bindings and previews.

Fixed-cohort acquisition stopping uses the selected cohort as its denominator,
including persisted failures before any next dispatch. The full pilot gate,
independent semantic support and training/promotion gates remain unchanged.

`transport: stream-all-v2` extends durable native streaming to teacher and
comparison requests as well as images. Their frozen request includes storage and
a binding tag; a retained creation event enables GET-only continuation after a
disconnect. Received answers keep the same typed scope/corroboration rules. Older
transport defaults and all saved requests remain unchanged. A connection lost
before acknowledgement can still be unknown: preserve its full reservation and
exclude its family/ancestry rather than send another POST.

Admission-window checks use the dispatch `admit` hook, before claiming a send
permit or recording a wire transition. Pure adapter validation may run again
inside execution. An expired admission leaves the frozen outbox reserved and
unsent; it must not be reported as an unknown provider outcome. A permit whose
wire transition failed can be released only while its state remains reserved,
with the matching token and an audit event. Sent/pending/unknown outcomes cannot
be reset this way.

Quota scans still count every regular private file and enforce the same disk
limit and free-space guard; directory entries avoid repeated filesystem stats.
Stop checks use a single campaign flag read, and status summaries use a covering
index containing the response ID rather than inline request/acceptance media.

`sam-sheet-v8` keeps full six-pose image generation and submits ONE spatial
six-object SAM request per whole screen. Every native cell passes the same raw
clipping preflight before that send; masks retain original per-cell background,
margin, alpha and scene-exclusion checks. Frame cutting and sequence assembly
are deterministic local derivations with retained originals. There is no paid
per-pose dispatch or fallback after a failed answer. Older frozen policies stay
unchanged; this opt-in must independently qualify under existing release gates.

A documented local preflight deadline denial can have its phantom worker slot
excluded through an explicit audit of the saved batch, pure adapter guard and
immediate event sequence without any provider observation. This does not change
its unknown state, full reserved spend, stopped study or permanent family
exclusion, and never authorizes a resend. Real unknown provider jobs still occupy
concurrency. This narrowly separates a proven local guard from an actual network
acceptance ambiguity.

`sam-whitespace-sheet-v9` uses a separately frozen extraction geometry for full
pose screens. It cuts only on observed technical-whitespace bands between rows
and between subjects within each row. Each original crop still passes the raw
2% margin check. All foreground RGB pixels are retained without resizing; only observed technical backing may be trimmed. Common
square cells are padded with explicitly recorded technical white, aligning the
original nominal centers and using a frozen1.5 nominal-cell view across every sheet in the sequence. One
whole-sheet SAM call then extracts the subjects. Mask margins,98% technical
surroundings and25% empty area are rechecked on the ORIGINAL UNPADDED crops, and
selecting synthetic padding fails. Padding cannot dilute a failed source gate.
Clipped canvas edges, overlaps, ambiguous/absent gutters and extra/scenic content
still reject. No earlier fixed-grid failure is reprocessed as an accepted result.
The neutral anchor brief uses only the frozen identity design and an independent
single-pose contract, never the shared animation/extraction instructions.
